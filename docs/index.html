How it works

Print. Pick. Done.

No new app to learn. It lives exactly where printing already lives — the Print dialog of every app on your Mac.

01 · PRINT

Hit Print

Choose Print@ Nearby, or a saved shop like “Print@ Staples.” Set what matters: price, turnaround, open now, closest.

02 · PICK

It finds the shop

It locates you, searches every network and local shop, checks hours and prices with AI, and shows you the best match with the cost.

03 · SENT

Off it goes

The PDF is emailed to the shop or the kiosk, a pickup code comes back, and you walk in and grab it. Often free at hotels.

Reach

Works with the whole planet's printers

Chains, kiosks, independents, hotel business centers, libraries and airport lounges — Print@ speaks to all of them, and picks between them for you.

🏪

The chains

FedEx Office, Staples, Office Depot and the UPS Store — sent to their central email-to-print, released at any kiosk with a code.

🏨

Hotels & libraries

3,200 PrinterOn printers worldwide. The lobby printer thirty feet away is often the closest — and free for guests.

🖨️

Kiosks

1,979 PrintMe locations — Office Depot, Staples, campuses — email a PDF, get a barcode, print at the machine.

📍

Local shops

The independent copy shop two blocks over that no network app can see. Print@ finds it on Apple Maps and ranks it too.

🧠

AI picks for you

It verifies today's hours, real prices and how each shop takes files — then ranks by what you asked for.

🔒

Yours, entirely

Runs on your Mac. Your files, your email, your rules. No account, no middleman, no cloud lock-in. MIT licensed.

Live directory

We mapped every public printer on Earth

Print@ ships the whole PrinterOn and PrintMe directories — pulled live, watched on a WOPR-style war-dialer board while it scans. 5,000+ locations, cached so nearby ones appear instantly.

Printers near you

Every print shop on one map

See what's around you at a glance — PrinterOn, PrintMe, chains and local shops, color-coded, with the address and exactly how the job would be sent.
