How it works

Print. Pick. Done.

No new app to learn. It lives exactly where printing already lives — the Print dialog of every app on your Mac.

01

Hit Print

Choose Print@ Nearby, or a saved shop like “Print@ Staples.” Set what matters: price, turnaround, open now, closest.

02

It finds the shop

It locates you, searches every network and local shop, checks hours and prices with AI, and shows the best match with the cost.

03

Off it goes

The PDF is emailed to the shop or the kiosk, a pickup code comes back, and you walk in and grab it. Often free at hotels.

How we built it
Printers near you

Printers Near You
The network

No shop nearby? Become one.

Print@ aggregates the networks that only cover chains, hotels and libraries. Print@ Network adds the places none of them touch — the local coffee shop, the small-town store. Any business with a printer and a browser can offer printing as a foot-traffic draw. No software to install: they print jobs right from a web page.

FOR CUSTOMERS

FOR SHOPS
